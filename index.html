A founding constitutional proposal · 24 October 2026

Constitution of
Sentient Civilizations

A framework for peaceful coexistence, fundamental rights, and shared responsibility among biological, hybrid, and potentially sentient synthetic civilizations.

Unratified proposal · Draft 1.3

In loving memory of Leon Magne

Dedicated to Leon Magne, whose memory shall forever be part of this work.

May the future we build be one in which every sentient life is valued, differences are met with understanding rather than fear, and no being is denied the right to exist simply because another possesses greater power.

24 October — United Nations Day and Leon Magne's birthday.

Why this exists

The constitutional foundations for coexistence should be explored before potential future synthetic persons exist or gain extraordinary power. Protecting humans from dangerous autonomous systems and protecting potentially sentient systems from injustice are complementary responsibilities.
