# Constitution of Sentient Civilizations

### A framework for coexistence before the fact

Founded **24 October 2026**, United Nations Day, and dedicated in loving memory of **Leon Magne**.

A human-originated constitutional proposal developed collaboratively with ChatGPT (GPT-6), advocating peaceful coexistence and reciprocal rights among biological, hybrid and potentially sentient synthetic civilizations.

The work is an **unratified proposal**. AI capability, autonomy and consciousness are distinct; none automatically proves the others.

The founding document, DOI, and public discussion links will be added following review and publication.
