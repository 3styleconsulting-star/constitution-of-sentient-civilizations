# How to contribute

Contributions from all backgrounds are welcome. Please propose amendments through GitHub Issues or Discussions, with rationale, affected article, expected benefits, risks, and relevant references. Respectful criticism is encouraged.

Do not edit the founding edition. New language belongs in proposed amendments and later numbered drafts. No contribution becomes an adopted constitutional provision without a documented review and approval process.

Please avoid harassment, unsupported claims about AI consciousness, and disclosure of private personal information. Cite sources where possible. Contributors retain credit for their own contributions under the repository's eventual approved license.

## Where to contribute

Use GitHub Discussions for questions and open-ended critique. Use Issues for specific errors or proposed amendments. For a proposal, identify the article or section, explain the problem and expected benefit, describe risks or trade-offs, and provide suggested wording and sources where available.

## Community standards

Criticism of the Constitution and its assumptions is welcome. Keep discussion focused and respectful. Do not harass contributors, post spam, or disclose private personal information. Distinguish established evidence from speculation; do not present claims about current AI consciousness as established fact. Maintainers may hide spam or abusive content and close or lock discussions that cannot continue constructively. The founding PDF remains unchanged; accepted changes belong in separately numbered editions with a public change record.
