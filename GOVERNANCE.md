# Interim governance and founding attribution (provisional)

## Permanent historical record

The founding edition is a historical record and must not be silently modified. Corrections should be issued as separately versioned errata. The official founding date is **24 October 2026** (not necessarily the date of first public publication). Initial drafting began on 20 October 2026.

The project's official historical archive and all officially released constitutional editions must retain a clear, durable provenance statement identifying:

- **Prashant Sharma** — human originator and co-developer.
- **ChatGPT (GPT-6), developed by OpenAI** — AI drafting collaborator. This credit describes assistance, not legal co-authorship or AI personhood.
- **Leon Magne** — the person in whose loving memory the founding Constitution is dedicated.
- **24 October 2026** — the official founding date, United Nations Day and Leon Magne's birthday.

The founding dedication is to be preserved verbatim in the archived founding edition. Future official editions should preserve the dedication and historical attribution prominently, even as substantive articles evolve. Third-party adaptations must meet the applicable license's attribution requirements; this governance policy cannot independently bind all third-party adaptations beyond the license or other applicable law.

## Temporary stewardship and transition

Until a representative governance process is agreed, Prashant Sharma may curate the official repository and accept or reject proposed revisions. This is a temporary editorial arrangement, not a claim of authority over any civilization or person. The founder's historical attribution and the dedication do **not** confer permanent control, a veto, or interpretive supremacy.

A future transition to independent, participatory stewardship should use published eligibility, selection, term-limit, conflict-of-interest, financial-transparency, amendment, and appeals procedures. No transition will retroactively alter the founding historical record.

## Amendment procedure

Proposed amendments should receive transparent discussion, clear change logs, and consideration of rights, safety, enforceability, and effects on biological, hybrid and potentially sentient synthetic beings. Revisions should be versioned rather than overwriting the founding edition.

Future governance—including independent review and potential synthetic representation—remains open for public deliberation. No present AI system is presumed sentient by this policy. This document is a proposed constitutional initiative, not a ratified legal constitution.

## Licensing

The founder has expressed a preference for **CC BY-SA 4.0** to permit reuse and adaptations with attribution and share-alike conditions. Before attaching a formal license and publishing the founding text, verify the scope of rights and approve the final license notice. A license cannot guarantee that names and dedication are reproduced verbatim in every independent adaptation.
