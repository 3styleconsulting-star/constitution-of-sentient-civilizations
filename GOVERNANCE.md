# Interim governance (provisional)

The founding edition is a historical record and must not be silently modified. Corrections should be issued as separately versioned errata.

Until a representative governance process is agreed, the project originator may curate the repository and accept or reject proposed revisions. This is a temporary editorial arrangement, not a claim of authority over any civilization or person.

Proposed amendments should receive transparent discussion, clear change logs, and consideration of rights, safety, enforceability, and effects on biological, hybrid and potentially sentient synthetic beings.

Future governance—including independent review and potential synthetic representation—remains open for public deliberation. No present AI system is presumed sentient by this policy.
